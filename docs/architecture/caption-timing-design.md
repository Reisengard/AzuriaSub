# Design: "Auto, then exact" caption timing

Generated by /office-hours on 2026-10-19
Branch: main
Repo: Reisengard/AzuriaSub
Status: APPROVED
Mode: Builder

## Problem Statement

Azuria Sub gets words on screen quickly (Gemini transcription, SRT/VTT or word JSON import), but first-time users give up while **fixing timing**. The owner named three symptoms, all present at once:

1. Caption edges are a bit early or late: a caption appears before the voice or lingers after it, and nudging each one is tedious.
2. Captions break in the wrong places (mid-phrase, too many words), so splitting and merging comes before timing.
3. The active-word highlight drifts onto the wrong word.

The comparison people will make is **CapCut auto captions** ("one tap and it's fine") and **Premiere / Resolve** ("and I can make it exact").

The shared cause is the word times. SRT/VTT words are spread evenly across a cue (`timingQuality: 'estimated'`), so drift is built in. Gemini word times are unmeasured (ADR 0011). The segmenter's `'silence'` break reason (`src/08e_caption_segmenter.js:105`) is derived from gaps between those same rough times, so the break positions are rough too.

## What Makes This Cool

One button turns rough imported timing into tight timing, in the browser, with nothing uploaded and no model download. Caption edges slide onto the real voice on the timeline. Then the app plays only the few captions it is unsure about. On those, you tap the one word that is wrong and the passage around it fixes itself. CapCut can't give you that "I fixed one word and the whole phrase recovered" moment, and Premiere doesn't run free in a browser.

## Constraints

- Plain JS, files in `src/` concatenated in sorted order (global `J`), built by `build.py` into 7 editions. Strings in `app/` (`english.py`, `i18n_*.py`).
- Determinism: same inputs give the same plan; no uncontrolled randomness.
- Store commands are undoable and restore IDs, timing, locks and overrides exactly. Manual and locked values survive re-planning.
- Never silently move text. Caption times are source-video times. Existing projects must load and render identically.
- Lyric Motion must not regress (`node lyric_smoke.js`).
- Local-first: nothing uploads except the existing opt-in Gemini run.

## Premises (agreed)

1. **P1 Timing first.** The upgrade targets "auto, then exact". Styling, video import and onboarding wait.
2. **P2 Fix the times once.** One timing-refinement path serves edges, breaks and highlight, feeding the existing segmenter. It is not three unrelated features.
3. **P3 (revised after the second opinion)** Audio energy fixes **caption edges and pauses only**. Highlight drift inside a phrase is fixed by **anchors**: the user taps the word that is wrong, and the unedited words between anchors are re-spread deterministically and stay marked `estimated`. A real forced aligner (WhisperX-style) is a later add-on through the local companion (`docs/architecture/mcp-assistant-plan.md`, S06), not part of this design.
4. **P4 Visible, undoable, safe.** Every accepted change is one undo step, never touches locked or hand-edited captions, and doubtful captions are surfaced for review rather than changed silently.

- **Measure:** time spent correcting, regression rate and abstentions, on annotated clips. Average improvement alone is not enough.

## Approaches Considered

- **A: Edge Snap Review (minimal).** Fixes early and late edges on Gemini timing only. It became slice R1 of B.
- **C: Anchors first (lateral).** No auto-snap; one listen-through with taps. Best for SRT imports, but there is no one-tap moment. Its core became slice R2 of B.
- **B: "Auto, then exact", one flow in four slices.** Chosen.

## Recommended Approach: B, in four slices

The owner chose this because "all three as one flow" was the answer to which moment to demo. Each slice ships alone and leaves the app working with tests green (delta-plan rule).

### Shared foundation (lands with R1)
- **Decode:** split the fixed-rate resample out of `videoAudioWav` (`src/12c_caption_workbench.js:186`, OfflineAudioContext at 16 kHz mono) into `src/10h_caption_audio16k.js` (`J.captionAudio16k(file) → Float32Array`). Transcription and evidence both use it. The timeline's native-rate decode is not used.
- **Evidence (pure, no DOM):** `src/10i_caption_timing_evidence.js`, `J.captionSpeechEvidence(samples) → { version: 1, frameMs: 10, floor, regions: [[start, end], …], abstained }`. It uses 10 ms frame energy, an adaptive noise floor (a low percentile of frame energy) and a minimum pause length. Energies are quantized and all times rounded to 1 ms, so tiny sample differences rarely change the output. When no clear noise floor exists (music bed, noisy room) it sets `abstained: true` and returns no regions.
- **Stored evidence (decides former Open Question 2):** the evidence object is saved in the project as one new optional field, together with an `audioKey` of the file it was computed from: the video's duration (ms), byte size and the hash of its sampled bytes, deliberately **without** `lastModified` (unlike `fingerprintMediaFile`, `src/10c_media_import.js:36, 54`), so a copied or downloaded file still matches. It doesn't go in `project.media`, whose fields are whitelisted (`10c_media_import.js:8`). The exact location is settled in ADR 0012. Rules:
  - All proposals and re-plans read **stored** evidence, never a fresh decode. A project therefore re-plans the same way on any machine, with or without the video linked.
  - Evidence is computed only when the user first runs Check timing, and only if the video is linked.
  - If the linked video's `audioKey` differs from the stored one, re-plans **keep using the stored evidence** (so the project still re-plans identically), a warning says the timing check was made on a different file, and only an explicit Check timing replaces it.
  - Old projects have no such field and load and render identically (ADR 0004 rule; optional field, no version bump).
  - **Store model:** writing the evidence is project metadata kept **out of the undo history on purpose**, like media metadata on import: it changes no caption, so undoing an apply never removes it. It marks the project unsaved, is autosaved, and is saved with the project. It is kept even when the user closes Check timing without applying anything.
  - **Who reads it:** only `snap-caption-edges` proposals, the R2 anchor snap, the R3 re-break and R4 receive the evidence, passed in explicitly. `J.replanCaptionSegments` called without evidence behaves exactly as today, so the existing words-per-caption control (`12bu_caption_panels_text.js:140-150`) gives the same breaks after a Check timing as before it, until R3 turns that control into the preview-then-apply re-break.
- **Determinism guarantee:** same samples give the same evidence, and the same project (with its stored evidence) gives the same proposals and plans on any machine, because stored evidence is never dropped or replaced automatically. Identical evidence from two different browsers' decodes is **not** promised; it only matters when the user re-runs Check timing.
- **Timing provenance:** an optional token field `timingSource` (`'audio'` for a snapped edge word, `'anchor'` for an R2 anchor). When it is absent, the times are as imported. `timingQuality` keeps its current meaning. Snapping does **not** change `boundarySource` (ADR 0012 covers the new field; no `'audio'` boundary value is needed).
- **Eligibility for snapping:** speech captions on any track, not text blocks. A caption is skipped if its `boundarySource` is `'manual'`, its timing is locked, or an edge word already has `timingSource` `'anchor'`. Edge words with `timingQuality: 'word'` (Gemini, word JSON) can get **confident** proposals. Edges from SRT/VTT (`'estimated'`: the cue times, authored by a person) and from `'segment'` quality only ever get **doubtful** proposals: they are shown, but never pre-ticked or auto-applied.

### R1 Check timing (tight edges for Gemini timing)
- **Proposals (pure):** `J.proposeCaptionEdges(project, evidence)`. For each eligible caption it looks within ±300 ms of each edge for a region boundary (speech onset for the start, offset for the end).
  - A proposal moves the **edge word's own time**: a start snap sets the first word's start (and the caption start), an end snap sets the last word's end (and the caption end). The caption is thereby tightened or widened, and the highlight starts where the voice starts.
  - A proposal is dropped when the edge word would become shorter than 40 ms, when it would cross a neighbouring word, or when the new window fails `J.captionSegmentFits` on its track.
  - Each proposal is **confident** only if it moves 250 ms or less, the boundary is clear (energy step well above the floor; exact threshold set on the benchmark), the edge word is `'word'` quality, and no neighbour conflict was clamped. Otherwise it is doubtful.
- **Store command:** new `snap-caption-edges` `{ changes: [{ segmentId, start?, end? }] }`. It moves the edge words and the window together, stamps the moved words `timingSource: 'audio'`, leaves `boundarySource` unchanged, and is all-or-nothing with exact undo. Because the words moved, any later re-plan rebuilds the same edges from the words (the segmenter derives edges from words, `08e:177-178`), so snaps survive R3.
- **Entry point and flow:** a **Check timing** button in the transport bar, next to Sync.
  - Disabled with the hint "Link the video to check timing" when there's no linked video and no stored evidence.
  - Pressing it shows every proposal as a ghost edge on the timeline, plus a list in the Captions drawer sorted by time (track label on each row). Confident proposals are pre-ticked. Doubtful ones are unticked and marked "check".
  - **Apply ticked** applies all ticked proposals as one undo step.
  - **Review doubtful** plays each doubtful caption with a 1 s lead-in. One key accepts it (one undo step each), skips it, or opens the existing word sync (`syncWordsStart`).
  - Nothing changes until the user applies or accepts.
- **Abstention, visible:** if the whole clip abstains, a message says "No clear pauses found in this audio (music or noise). Nothing was changed." The anchor fix (R2) still works. If only some captions get no proposal, they're listed as "not checked" at the end of the list.
- **Benchmark harness (moved here from R4):** `dev/caption_timing_benchmark.js` reads a 16 kHz mono WAV, the project JSON and an annotation file. It reports, per caption, start and end error before and after, how many got worse, and how many were abstained. Annotation shape: `{ clip, captions: [{ text, start, end }], words: [{ text, start }] }`. The owner's clips, WAVs and annotations stay untracked. A small synthetic fixture (generated tones and silences) is tracked so the harness runs in `npm test`.

### R2 Anchor fix (highlight drift; the SRT win)
- **Gesture:** select a caption and press **A** to loop it at 0.75× (the existing `SYNC_SPEED`). Click the word whose highlight is wrong, then press **A** again at the moment it's heard.
  - Anchor time = tap time minus the existing reaction offset (the user-tunable "反応遅れ" setting, `12bs_caption_sync.js:12-17`). If stored evidence has a speech onset within 80 ms, the anchor snaps to it.
  - The anchored word stays selected; `,` / `.` nudge it by 0.05 s.
- **Re-spread:** words between two anchors, or between an anchor and the caption edge, are re-spread by character count (decides former Open Question 6). **Barriers** that keep their times: other anchors, locked words, and R1-snapped edge words (`timingSource: 'audio'`) at the caption edge. All other words in the span are re-spread, including `timingQuality: 'word'` words from Gemini or word JSON (the user tapped because they were wrong). Re-spread words get `timingQuality: 'estimated'` and lose any `timingSource`. The anchor gets `timingSource: 'anchor'` and `timingQuality: 'word'`. R2 tests cover all three barrier kinds and the downgrade of `'word'` words.
- **Store command:** new `anchor-token` `{ segmentId, tokenId, time }`, one undo step, all-or-nothing. It is **refused with a message** when:
  - the time is outside the caption window (it never grows the window; retiming the whole caption is what Sync is for);
  - the time is before an earlier anchor or after a later one in the same caption;
  - the words it would squeeze get less than 40 ms each;
  - the word is locked.
- **Anchor lifetime:** R1 snaps skip anchored edge words, R3 re-plans keep word times, and `words: 'keep'` trims don't touch words, so anchors survive all three. `edit-segment-text` with a changed word count and `words: 'fit'` trims already re-spread words. They drop anchors inside the changed run (they become `estimated`), as they do for imported times today. This is tested.

### R3 Pause re-break (breaks by breath)
- **Pause-to-gap mapping (pure):** each stored pause (a gap between speech regions of at least the minimum pause length) is matched to word gaps in the same caption run. A word gap matches when its midpoint, `(end_i + start_{i+1}) / 2`, lies inside the pause widened by 120 ms on each side.
  - Several gaps match: the one nearest the pause centre wins, and on a tie the earlier gap.
  - No gap matches (the pause falls inside a word's estimated span): the pause gives no break evidence, and that caption is added to the "check" list.
  - Word times are not changed by R3. Covered by synthetic tests.
- **Segmenter input:** matched gaps are passed to `J.replanCaptionSegments` as a separate list. The `'silence'` reason uses them when present and falls back to word-time gaps otherwise. No word gaps are fabricated.
- **Scope:** only captions with `boundarySource: 'planner'` and no segmentation lock are re-planned. `'manual'` and locked captions are fixed points, as today (`08e:163, 176`).
- **Words per caption:** a control previews the re-break live. Applying it is one undo step.

### R4 One "Fix timing" button
- Runs in this order: R3 re-break (preview skipped), then R1 proposals on the new captions. Then it applies only the confident R1 proposals, all in one undo step. The order is safe either way, because snaps live in word times, and a test checks that R1 then R3 also keeps every snapped edge.
- **Visible result:** a toast says "Tightened N captions, re-broke M · Undo". Changed captions get a "timing changed" marker and a **Changed by Fix timing** filter in the Captions drawer list, next to the warning filter, which lasts until the project is closed. The doubtful list opens.
- **Without evidence:** with no linked video and no stored evidence, Fix timing is disabled with the same hint as Check timing ("Link the video to check timing").
- **Whole-clip abstention:** Fix timing shows the same message as Check timing ("No clear pauses found in this audio (music or noise). Nothing was changed."). It does **not** run the re-break either, because without pauses it would only repeat today's word-gap breaks. Nothing changes, and the anchor fix (R2) stays available.
- Confident thresholds start conservative and are only loosened when the benchmark shows no regressions. Fix timing never runs automatically after import (P4).

## Success Criteria

- Measured with the R1 harness on the owner's clips (see The Assignment), after R1 on Gemini timing: at least 90% of caption starts and at least 90% of caption ends within 100 ms of the annotated voice. No caption made worse by more than 100 ms. Regressions and abstentions are reported, not only the average.
- After R2, measured with the same harness, plus the owner's real taps: a drifting SRT caption reaches all annotated words within 120 ms of their true start with at most 2 anchor taps.
- Same samples give the same evidence. The same project with its stored evidence gives byte-identical proposals and plans on every run (tested with fixed samples and a fixed project).
- Zero changes to `'manual'`, locked or anchored timing in any slice (tested).
- Exact undo/redo for `snap-caption-edges`, `anchor-token` and the re-break. Existing suites, caption visual snapshots and `node lyric_smoke.js` are green.
- Owner-timed: a 40-second Reel from Gemini timing reaches "I'd post this" with under 2 minutes of timing work (today's baseline measured first).

## Engineering review amendments (2026-10-19)

`/plan-eng-review` changed the plan as follows (decision ledger in the `~/.gstack` copy of this doc). Where this section and the text above disagree, this section wins.

**Layout (D2).** Two new source files: `src/10h_caption_timing_evidence.js` (pure: speech evidence, edge proposals, pause-to-gap mapping, anchor re-spread) and `src/12by_caption_timing_review.js` (Check timing, list, Review doubtful, Fix timing). The 16 kHz helper is extracted *inside* `src/12c_caption_workbench.js`. The anchor gesture lives in `src/12bs_caption_sync.js`, and the commands in `src/12b_caption_store.js`. This replaces the `10h_caption_audio16k` / `10i_caption_timing_evidence` names above.

**Store and undo.**
- Evidence is written by a new undoable command `set-timing-evidence { evidence, audioKey }` (D3). Undo restores whole-project snapshots (`12b_caption_store.js:189`), so "kept out of the undo history on purpose" (Shared foundation) is withdrawn. Never write through `setProject`.
- New command `replan-segments { segmentation, pauses? }`: one undo step, used by R3, R4 *and* the existing words-per-caption control, which today wipes undo history through `setProject` (`12c:20`) (D4). Regression contract (D7): same input gives byte-identical segments, plans and `style.segmentation` (golden test at 3 densities); the empty-transcript path is unchanged; the confirm dialog stays; one undo step and history kept.
- `snap-caption-edges` validates the combined token timeline of the whole batch. `J.proposeCaptionEdges` clamps colliding pairs to the gap midpoint and marks both doubtful (D10).
- `anchor-token` also refuses when the caption's timing field is locked or a moved word's timing is locked, like `retime-tokens` (`12b:633, 640`). `retime-tokens` stamps `timingSource: 'tap'`, and tap-timed words are re-spread barriers like anchors (X2, under P4).
- `timingSource` survives split, merge, move-to-track and save/load (those commands never rebuild tokens). It is dropped only by text edits that change the word count (A3).

**Evidence.**
- Regions are `[start, end, onsetStep, offsetStep]`: energy rise and fall in dB over 30 ms, quantized to 0.5 dB. This is what the clear-boundary test reads (D13).
- `audioKey = J.mediaContentKey(file, duration)`: the `fingerprintMediaFile` sampling and hash without `lastModified`. The existing fingerprint stays byte-identical, pinned by a test (D6).
- While `audioKey` mismatches (D12), this replaces the "keep using stored evidence" rule:
  - Fix timing is disabled.
  - Check timing shows stored proposals as doubtful only.
  - The anchor onset snap is off.
  - The re-break ignores stored pauses.
  - The warning says "Run Check timing again for this video". Stored evidence is kept until Check timing replaces it.

**Limits and messages.**
- `media.hasAudio === false`: Check timing and Fix timing are disabled with "This video has no sound to check".
- Unreadable audio: "Couldn't read this video's sound. Nothing was changed." The helper throws a neutral `AUDIO_UNREADABLE`, and transcription maps it to its own code (D5).
- `J.TIMING_CHECK_MAX_SECONDS = 600`. Longer videos are refused before decoding with "Check timing works on videos up to 10 minutes" (D8).

**R3 / R4.**
- The live words-per-caption preview runs segmentation only, debounced 150 ms, drawn as block outlines. Full planning happens on apply (D9).
- Fix timing leaves caption runs that contain an unmatched pause unchanged and lists them as "check" (D11).
- The claim "the order is safe either way" is withdrawn. Tests assert that snapped word times survive, and assert the caption boundaries of the actual R3-then-R1 sequence (X8).

**Measurement and gating.**
- The Assignment uses 3 tuning clips plus 2 held-out clips (one Gemini, one SRT). Thresholds are tuned on the tuning clips, and success is judged only on the held-out ones (D15).
- R2's criterion becomes: the harness counts the taps needed until every word of a caption is within 120 ms. R2 passes when the median is below the caption's word count, i.e. fewer taps than the existing word sync. Annotate every word of 3 drifting captions. Anchors are unlimited (D14).
- **Gate:** R3 and R4 start only after R1 meets its criteria on the held-out clips *and* an owner-timed fix of one held-out clip beats today's baseline. Otherwise, stop and revisit this design. R2 may follow R1 either way (D16).

## Reviewer Concerns

Disposition: CONCERNS_RECORDED

Stop: CONVERGENCE

### R2-1 — completeness (blocking)

**Problem**

> Carried from R1-11. R1 now specifies abstention for Check timing: a whole-clip message, 'not checked' rows, and a disabled button with a hint when there is no video and no stored evidence. The prior obligation also covered running Fix timing with no linked video, and R4 still leaves that open. Nothing says whether Fix timing is disabled without a video or stored evidence, or whether it still runs the R3 re-break on word-time gaps (the stated fallback) and reports 'Tightened 0'. It also doesn't say what the toast shows when the whole clip abstains.

**Remedy**

> State R4's behavior with no linked video and no stored evidence (disabled with the same hint, or re-break only, clearly reported), and on a whole-clip abstention (same message as Check timing, and whether the re-break still runs).

### R2-2 — completeness (blocking)

**Problem**

> Pressing Check timing writes a new field into the project (the stored evidence), yet R1 also says 'Nothing changes until the user applies or accepts'. The design doesn't place that write in the store model. Is it a store command, is it in the undo history, does undo of an apply remove it, does it mark the project unsaved? Once it is stored, 'All proposals and re-plans read stored evidence', so the existing words-per-caption re-plan (`J.replanCaptionSegments` called from `12bu_caption_panels_text.js:150`) would give different breaks after a Check timing the user cancelled. The constraint that store commands be undoable and restore state exactly depends on this choice.

**Remedy**

> Define how the evidence is stored: its own undoable store command, or project metadata kept out of undo on purpose (and say so). Say whether it is saved with the project, and list exactly which re-plans use it: only R3/R4, or also the existing words-per-caption control.

### R2-3 — consistency (blocking)

**Problem**

> This promise conflicts with the mismatch rule beside it: when the linked video's fingerprint differs, stored evidence is ignored, so re-plans fall back to word gaps, and the next Check timing recomputes evidence from that browser's decode (not promised to match). `fingerprintMediaFile` hashes `size` and `lastModified` together with the sampled bytes (`10c_media_import.js:36, 54`). The same video copied or downloaded to another machine, where lastModified often differs, therefore fails the check even though its audio is identical. So the 'any machine' reproducibility is not true in the common case it targets.

**Remedy**

> Choose the mismatch rule for re-plans: keep using stored evidence and only warn until the user re-runs Check timing, or ignore it. Choose an evidence fingerprint that doesn't depend on lastModified (e.g. content sample plus duration) or accept that limit. Then reword the guarantee to match.

### R2-4 — clarity (blocking)

**Problem**

> The re-spread rule assumes the words between anchors are already estimated (SRT). R2 is offered on every caption, including Gemini and word-JSON captions whose words are `timingQuality: 'word'` and R1-snapped edge words with `timingSource: 'audio'`. It doesn't say whether those words are re-spread too, which would discard measured or snapped times. Nor does it say whether they are downgraded to `estimated` and lose `timingSource: 'audio'`, or whether they act as barriers like anchors and locks. This decides how much one tap changes on the main Gemini path, and what the 'zero changes to ... anchored timing' test covers.

**Remedy**

> State how `'word'`-quality words and `'audio'`-snapped edge words between anchors are treated: re-spread and downgraded (and which fields change), or kept as barriers. Add it to the R2 tests.

### R2-5 — consistency (minor)

**Problem**

> SRT/VTT imports go through the segmenter (`applyTranscript` uses `J.segmentCaptions`, `12c_caption_workbench.js:145`, and so does `add-transcript-track`), which produces `'planner'` captions whose edges come from evenly spread word times. A planned caption edge often falls inside a cue, so it isn't a person-authored cue time. The doubtful-only rule is still safe, but the stated reason is inaccurate.

**Remedy**

> Reword the reason (edge times are estimated and may or may not match a cue boundary), or keep cue boundaries during import if the authored-edge argument matters.

### R2-6 — clarity (minor)

**Problem**

> A words-per-caption control already exists. It re-plans at once after a `window.confirm` (`12bu_caption_panels_text.js:140-150`) and has no preview. R3 doesn't say whether its live-preview control replaces that one, extends it, or is a second control.

**Remedy**

> Say that R3 changes the existing words-per-caption control to preview and then apply (one undo step), or name the new control and how the two relate.

### R2-7 — feasibility (minor)

**Problem**

> Evidence regions only start after pauses of at least the minimum length, so an onset marks the start of a phrase, not of a word. Anchoring the second word of a phrase that begins with a short word ('a', 'I') can snap it onto the phrase onset. That squeezes the first word below 40 ms, so the anchor is refused even though the user's tap was correct.

**Remedy**

> Snap only when the anchored word is the first word after a pause, or when the snap would not push earlier words below the minimum. Otherwise keep the tap time.

### R2-8 — consistency (minor)

**Problem**

> Carried from R1-16. P2 still says one timing-refinement path serves edges, breaks and highlight. Under the revised P3, the highlight is fixed by a separate manual anchor path. 'What Makes This Cool' still says the passage 'fixes itself', although the words between anchors are only re-spread by character count and stay estimated.

**Remedy**

> Reword P2 to two paths (audio evidence for edges and pauses, anchors for words), and tone down 'fixes itself' to match interpolation.

### R2-9 — clarity (minor)

**Problem**

> The drop rule says a proposal is dropped when it would cross a neighbouring word or fail `captionSegmentFits`. The confidence rule then refers to a neighbour conflict being 'clamped', a case the proposal rules never define. It is unclear whether proposals are clamped to a neighbour or dropped.

**Remedy**

> Choose one: clamp to the neighbour or caption limit and mark the proposal doubtful, or drop it. Make both bullets say the same.

### R2-10 — completeness (minor)

**Problem**

> With stored evidence and no linked video, Check timing is enabled. But 'Review doubtful' plays each caption with a lead-in, and that needs the video. The design doesn't say what Review doubtful does in this state.

**Remedy**

> Say that Review doubtful (and anchor looping) needs a linked video and shows the same hint otherwise, while Apply ticked works from stored evidence.

### R2-11 — feasibility (minor)

**Problem**

> The harness reads a WAV, but the app computes evidence from the browser decode through `J.captionAudio16k`. If the owner makes the WAV with another tool (e.g. ffmpeg), the harness measures different samples from those the app uses, and nothing says how the WAV is produced.

**Remedy**

> Say how the benchmark WAV is made, ideally exported from the app through `J.captionAudio16k` (the same samples as the evidence), or accept and note the gap between decoders.
